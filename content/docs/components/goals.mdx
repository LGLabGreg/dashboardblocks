title: Goals
description: Progress towards targets, with pace against where you should be by now.

Pace compares progress with a straight line from zero to the target over the period. Within 5% of that line counts as on track. Use it for values that build up over the period, such as revenue or deals closed, not for rates.

### Target

One target with an expected-by-today marker, what's left and a projection at the current pace.

### Goal List

Several goals for the same period, each with its own pace.

### Projection

Cumulative progress this month, projected to the end of the month at the current daily rate, against the target line.

### Daily Rings

Small daily goals as rings, with a check mark once a goal is met.
