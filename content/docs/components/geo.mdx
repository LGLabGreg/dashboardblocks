The maps need no map library or data files: US states are a grid of equal square tiles, so small states are as easy to read as large ones, and the world map is a dotted land mask of about 1.3 KB embedded in the component. Hover, focus or tap a tile or marker to read it under the map; with the keyboard, Tab into the map and use the arrow keys, Home and End. Every map has a legend and gives screen readers its data as a table. Flags are emoji, which some platforms, such as Windows, draw as two letters.
