Retention follows each cohort, such as everyone who signed up in March, from the period they joined. Pass a cohort's size and how many were still active in each period since, starting with period 0. Newer cohorts have fewer periods. Averages are weighted by cohort size, so a small cohort doesn't swing them. Changes are in percentage points, not percent.

### Cohort Table

A retention triangle with one row per cohort and a weighted average row. Cells are shaded against the best share after period 0, so period 0's 100% doesn't wash out the rest. Hover a cell to read its count.
