Flows show how a total splits and moves between stages. Nodes sit in columns and bands between them are as wide as the flow. A node's value is the larger of what flows in and out. Downstream nodes take the colour of their largest source, so a flow keeps its hue, and drop-offs are drawn muted. Hover a node or band to highlight what it connects. Each chart comes with a table of its links for screen readers, and scrolls sideways in narrow cards to keep labels legible.
