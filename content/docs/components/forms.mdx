---
title: Forms
description: Settings sections with a save bar, a form in a side sheet, a multi-step wizard and inline editing. Built on shadcn Field, with no form library.
---

The fields use shadcn's [Field](https://ui.shadcn.com/docs/components/field). State lives in `useSimpleForm`, a small hook in the primitives. It validates a field when it loses focus and the whole form on submit, focuses the first invalid field, and shows errors your server returns.

To use React Hook Form or TanStack Form instead, keep the layout and swap `useSimpleForm` for your library's hook. The fields take the same `id`, `name`, `value`, `onChange`, `onBlur` and `aria-invalid` props.
