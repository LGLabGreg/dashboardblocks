Insights explain what the data shows in a sentence, with the numbers that matter highlighted inline. Each insight has a kind (positive, negative, neutral or anomaly) that describes the outcome, not the direction, so churn falling is positive. Every kind comes with an icon and a label, and highlighted metrics say their direction in words, so colour never carries the meaning alone. The blocks render the text you pass them: generate it however you like, on the server or ahead of time.

### Feed

A list of insights, each with its kind, the topic, the sentence with its metrics highlighted, an optional "why" line of drivers and a link to explore.

### Anomaly Callout

An unusual reading against the range it was expected to fall in, with the days outside it highlighted, the explanation and the likely causes.

### Key Drivers

What contributed to a change, as bars either side of zero. The summary sentence is built from the numbers: the change, the biggest driver and the biggest drag.

<ComponentPreview name='insights-03' previewClassName='max-w-2xl' />

### Weekly Digest

A headline, three takeaways and helpful or not helpful buttons. The buttons are toggles: press one again to take the feedback back.
