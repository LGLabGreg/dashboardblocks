title: Gauge

A gauge is for one number on a known scale, where the bands or the target matter as much as the value. Each gauge is a `meter` with its range and a text value for screen readers, and every band comes with a label and an icon, so colour never carries it alone. Bands sit on the track when you mark the value with a knob, or as a thin scale inside the track when the value is a fill. The fill animates in once, and not at all with reduced motion.

### Net Promoter Score

NPS on its full −100 to 100 scale, filled from zero, with the split of promoters, passives and detractors behind it.
