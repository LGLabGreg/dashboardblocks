---
title: Record Detail
description: Pages for one customer, order or ticket. Headline numbers, related records, a timeline and a column of properties.
full: true
---

A record's page puts the main content first and a narrower column of properties beside it. The column moves below the content when the page is narrow, measured by the page's own width. The headers are built from [Page Header](/docs/components/page-header) and the timelines from [Activity Feed](/docs/components/activity-feed).

### Customer

Headline numbers, recent orders, contact details and notes.

### Order

Line items with totals, a timeline of what happened, the customer, and shipping and payment.
