Feedback blocks summarise what customers say. Ratings average from the count of each star, and CSAT is the share rating 4 or 5 out of 5, as it's usually reported. Sentiment uses red for negative, grey for neutral and green for positive, always with a legend and the numbers as text. Stars fill to fractions, with the rating read out for screen readers.

### Ratings

The average rating with stars, the number of ratings and the share of each star, with the change on the last period.
