title: Page Header
description: The top of a page. A heading with actions, section tabs, a record's header with a back link and facts, and a list page's search.
---

The heading is the page's `<h1>`. Actions sit beside it on wide headers and wrap under it on narrow ones, measured by the header's own width. Links are plain `<a>` elements, so render your router's link for client-side navigation.
